Tetric N-Ceram Bulk Fill

Order on WhatsApp
One layer. Up to four millimetres.
IVA IVB IVW

Tetric N-Ceram Bulk Fill is the efficient four-millimetres posterior composite of the nano-optimized Tetric N-Collection.

Tetric N-Ceram Bulk Fill enables posterior teeth to be restored with only one layer measuring up to four-millimetres in thickness, which considerably heightens efficiency.

The patented light activator Ivocerin is responsible for ensuring the complete cure of the filling. Compared with conventional light initiators, the Ivocerin polymerisation booster is much more reactive. Therefore, polymerisation is initiated even in very deep cavities and the material is fully cured.

A specially conditioned shrinkage stress reliever keeps shrinkage and shrinkage stress during polymerisation to a minimum.

Tetric N-Ceram Bulk Fill is available in three universal shades: IVA, IVB and IVW (universal A shade, universal B shade and for deciduous teeth or light-coloured permanent dentition).

Advantages
  • Bulk Fill is possible due to Ivocerin, the patented light initiator
How to use
  • Prepare the cavity and apply a suitable adhesive system.
  • Place a single increment up to 4 mm thick.
  • Light-cure per Ivoclar guidelines (about 10 s with a high-power lamp).
  • Finish and polish, then check the occlusion.
Additional information
  • Bulk-fill nano-hybrid composite for posterior restorations.
  • Up to 4 mm depth of cure in a single increment.
  • Sculptable with a controlled working time.
